## Part 5

### Definition Choices and Justifications

1. **Sold or Paid?**
   - **Choice:** Sold (using `order_items.price`).
   - **Reason:** Pure merchandise sales represent the true underlying business volume generated by sellers on the platform.

2. **Freight?**
   - **Choice:** Excluded (`freight_value` is not counted).
   - **Reason:** Freight is a pass-through delivery cost paid to logistics carriers and does not constitute net revenue for Olist or its sellers.

3. **Which Orders?**
   - **Choice:** Exclude `canceled` and `unavailable` orders.
   - **Reason:** Canceled or unfulfilled orders do not result in completed commercial transactions or realized value.

4. **Vouchers?**
   - **Choice:** Not applicable.
   - **Reason:** By anchoring our definition directly on merchandise item prices (`order_items.price`), coupon/voucher financing mechanics do not distort product pricing.

5. **Which Date & Timezone?**
   - **Choice:** `order_purchase_timestamp` in `America/Sao_Paulo` timezone.
   - **Reason:** It marks the exact point in time when the customer committed to the transaction locally.
